# How to Complete business-case.md

The Business Case answers *should we do this at all?* The Spec answers *what exactly are we building?* Write this first, in Week 1, keep it to about one page, and fill it in section by section — the hints in the template tell you what each part needs. Write the Constitution (`specification.md` Section 0) the same week.

For a filled-in version, see `docs/examples/business-case-example.md`.

---

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Leaving out "Do nothing" — without it, there's nothing to compare against.
- ❌ A problem statement that's really a solution ("We need an app").
- ❌ "Success" that's a feature list instead of something you can measure.
- ❌ Forgetting ongoing costs, such as hosting after the free tier runs out.
- ❌ Inventing precise benefit numbers you don't have. Say which benefits are measurable and which are real but hard to measure.
- ❌ A recommendation that doesn't commit ("We could explore a few options").
